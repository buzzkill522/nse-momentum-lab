What the bot does

    What the words mean

    Practice money
    The bot starts with a pretend ₹10,00,000. No real orders are placed.
    Total gain
    How much the pretend portfolio is up or down since the start, after trading costs.
    Index
    The average of the market. If the bot's gain is higher than the index, it is beating the market.
    Invested
    The share of the money that is in stocks. The rest is kept as cash.
    Times
    All times are Indian Standard Time. Prices come from Yahoo Finance and can be about 15 minutes behind.
