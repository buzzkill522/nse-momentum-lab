          What the words mean

          • Practice money: each bot starts with a pretend ₹10,00,000. No real orders are placed.
          • Total gain: how much the pretend portfolio is up or down since the start. The live simulation charges no trading fees.
          • Index: the average of the market. A bot ahead of the index is beating the market.
          • In stocks + Cash = Total: the total is the current value of the shares held plus the cash that has not been invested.
          • Biggest drop: the largest fall from a high point to a later low point.
          • Times: all Indian Standard Time. Prices come from Yahoo Finance and can be about 15 minutes behind.
